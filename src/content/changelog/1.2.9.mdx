title: "Dock icon drop, Canon HIF support and SVG fixes"
pubDate: 2025-12-10
---

You can now drag and drop files directly onto the Picmal dock icon to start a conversion instantly — no need to open the window first. Files also persist when you switch between the Convert and Compress tabs, so you won't lose your selection mid-workflow.

This version adds HIF format support for Canon cameras that shoot HEIF, and fixes a DNG/RAW rotation issue that caused some photos to appear sideways after conversion.

We also resolved a bug where SVG compression failed on files containing certain CSS class names.
