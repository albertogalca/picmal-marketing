Picmal 1.3.0 is a big one. You can now set up watched folders that automatically convert or compress any file you drop into them — perfect for photographers and designers who process files throughout the day.

The app also integrates with the macOS Shortcuts app, so you can build custom automations that trigger Picmal conversions from any workflow. And with the new background mode, Picmal starts quietly when you log in and stays ready in the menu bar.

Compression output is now available for images, audio, and video files.
