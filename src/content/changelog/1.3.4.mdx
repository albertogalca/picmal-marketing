title: "Stability fix for corrupted files"
pubDate: 2026-01-19
---

Fixed an issue where Picmal could become unresponsive when processing complex or corrupted files. The app now handles these gracefully and moves on to the next file in the queue.

Sentry error reporting has been improved to capture more context around failures, which helps us diagnose and ship fixes faster. Other minor stability improvements are included.
