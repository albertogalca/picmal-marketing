Picmal is a macOS app for converting and compressing images, audio, and video — entirely on your Mac. No files leave your machine, no internet required, no Homebrew needed.

Drop your files in, pick your settings, and go.

## Keyboard Shortcuts

| Shortcut | Action               |
| -------- | -------------------- |
| Cmd+O    | Add files or folders |
| Cmd+,    | Open Settings        |
| Cmd+Q    | Quit Picmal          |
