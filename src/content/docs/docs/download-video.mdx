Picmal's sidebar has a **Download Video** tool. Paste links, pick a folder, and Picmal fetches the videos to your Mac.

## Download a batch

1. Select **Download Video** in the sidebar
2. Add links — click **Add Links** and paste one per line, press ⌘V in the window, or drag a link straight from your browser's address bar
3. Choose where the files go (Picmal starts on your Downloads folder and remembers your choice)
4. Pick a quality for the batch
5. Click **Download**

![Picmal's Download Video tool with a finished 4K download marked Done, a QuickTime preview of the clip open over the list, and the destination folder and 1080p quality picker in the bottom bar](/images/docs/download_video.webp)

Each row shows its own progress, download speed, and estimated time remaining. When the batch finishes, the bottom bar says how many videos were saved and gives you a **Show in Finder** link.

## Quality

One picker sets the quality for every link in the list.

| Option     | What you get                                                        |
| ---------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Best       | The highest resolution the link offers, as an MP4. Default.         |
| 1080p      | Capped at 1080p, for a smaller file on a 4K source.                 |
| Audio only | The audio track on its own, with no video and no re-encode.         |

## Files that actually play

Sites hand out AV1 and VP9, and QuickTime refuses both with "contains some media that isn't compatible". Picmal checks every finished file: if the container can simply be rewrapped it does that, and if the footage really has to change it re-encodes — the row says **Converting** while it works. Files that already play are left exactly as they arrived.

Audio-only downloads pick an audio stream directly rather than extracting one, so there's no re-encode and no quality loss.

## When a link fails

The row tells you why in plain words rather than handing you raw tool output: the video is private or needs a sign-in, it isn't available in your region, the link is dead, the site is rate-limiting you, the disk is full, or Picmal can't save to that folder. Failed rows don't stop the rest of the batch.

Cancel a download and Picmal cleans up after itself — no half-written files left next to your finished videos.

## Fair use

Download Video is for content you have the right to save: your own uploads, material you've licensed, and videos whose terms allow it. Check the site's terms before you download, and don't use it on copy-protected streams — those are refused anyway.

Download Video is app-only for now. There's no Finder, Shortcuts, or command-line equivalent yet.

Like Convert and Compress, it requires an active [license](/docs/license).
