title: "Metadata controls, iCloud Drive support and HEVC video fixes"
pubDate: 2026-02-02
---

You now have full control over how metadata is handled during conversion. Choose between three modes: strip all metadata, keep only essentials (orientation, colour profile and copyright), or preserve everything. This is especially useful if you need to protect privacy while keeping colour accuracy.

File conversion now works reliably on iCloud Drive, network shares and read-only locations — no more "permission denied" errors when your files live outside the local disk.

HEVC videos with unusual frame rates now convert correctly to MOV and play properly in QuickTime. Other minor improvements are included.
