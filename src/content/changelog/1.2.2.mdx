title: "Advanced mode fixes, cancel button and progress indicator"
pubDate: 2025-11-04
---

The Advanced mode checkbox is now clickable again, and Advanced mode has been added to both Conversion and Compression modes. The bottom tab bar now truncates text properly when there's too much to display.

Progress indicators have been added to compression and conversion — especially helpful for long files that can take several minutes. A cancel button lets you stop the process at any time. Some PDF compression errors have also been fixed.
