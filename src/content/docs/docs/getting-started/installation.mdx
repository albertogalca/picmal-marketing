# Installation

Getting Picmal up and running on your Mac is quick and easy. Follow these steps to install the app.

## System Requirements

Before installing, make sure your Mac meets these requirements:

- **macOS Version**: macOS 11 (Big Sur) or later
- **Processor**: Apple Silicon (M1/M2/M3) or Intel processor
- **Storage**: At least 50 MB of free disk space
- **RAM**: 4 GB minimum (8 GB recommended for video conversion)

## Download Picmal

1. Visit the [Picmal homepage](https://picmal.app)
2. Click the **"Buy for Mac"** button
3. Complete your purchase through our secure checkout
4. You'll receive a download link via email immediately

## Install the App

Once you've downloaded the Picmal DMG file:

1. **Open the DMG file** - Double-click the downloaded `Picmal.dmg` file
2. **Drag to Applications** - Drag the Picmal icon to your Applications folder
3. **Launch Picmal** - Open your Applications folder and double-click Picmal

## First Launch

When you first launch Picmal, macOS may show a security prompt since the app was downloaded from the internet:

1. Click **"Open"** when prompted
2. If you don't see this option, go to **System Settings > Privacy & Security**
3. Look for the message about Picmal and click **"Open Anyway"**

## Activate Your License

After launching Picmal for the first time:

1. The license activation window will appear
2. Enter the license key from your purchase email
3. Click **"Activate"** to unlock all features

That's it! You're ready to start converting images and videos.

## Troubleshooting

**Can't open the app?**
- Make sure you're running macOS 11 or later
- Check System Settings > Privacy & Security for security blocks

**Lost your license key?**
- Check your email for the purchase confirmation
- Contact support with your purchase email address

**Need to reinstall?**
- Your license key works on up to 3 Macs
- Simply reinstall and enter your existing license key
