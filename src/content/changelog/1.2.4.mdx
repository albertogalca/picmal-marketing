You can now see the conversion speed percentage and estimated time remaining for video and audio files. Video conversions are much faster and use less memory, especially for long files.

Instant conversion (no re-encoding) now works for over 50 format combinations including WebM, MKV, MP4, FLV, 3GP and more. VideoToolbox H.264 hardware encoding is available on Apple Silicon for 12+ video formats, offering 10-50x speed improvements for FLV, WMV, MPEG, 3GP and others.

WebM codec compatibility in Advanced mode has been fixed, buttons are now clickable during conversion, and PDF compression is faster and more accurate.
