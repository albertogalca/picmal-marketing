Until now the only way to find out whether Picmal handles your files was to buy it and lean on the 14-day refund. That's backwards. From this release the download comes with a week of the full app, started from a button inside it, and the Buy button waits at the end.

### New features

- **Try Picmal free for 7 days.** Open the app and press **Try free** in the sidebar. Every tool runs on that Mac for a week: no key, no card, no account. The trial belongs to the Mac, so a reinstall picks up the same one, and when it ends the Buy button is right there. The command line, Shortcuts, Raycast, and watched folders still need a license.
