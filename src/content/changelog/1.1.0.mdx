Picmal now has a dedicated compress mode. Reduce the size of JPG, WebP, PNG, GIF, TIFF, AVIF and PDF files directly — without converting them to another format first.

You can also enable compression as a post-conversion step, so images are automatically optimised after being converted. Other minor improvements and bug fixes are included.
