## What's included in every license
- Batch convert and compress images, videos, audio, and PDFs
- 30+ image formats (HEIC, HEIF, JPG, PNG, WebP, AVIF, TIFF, BMP, GIF, SVG, ICO, ICNS, PDF, JXL, DNG/RAW, and more)
- Video conversion and compression (MP4, MOV, MKV, WebM, HEVC, and more), with hardware H.264/HEVC encoding
- Audio conversion and compression (MP3, WAV, FLAC, AAC, M4A, M4B, OGG, OPUS, and more)
- PDF compression that keeps text selectable, plus combine, split, images to PDF, and organize pages
- Video tools: combine clips, photo slideshow to MP4
- Audio tools: merge audio, build chaptered M4B audiobooks, split by chapter
- Vectorize raster images into real SVG paths
- Remove Background onto a transparent PNG
- App Icons: macOS .icns, Windows .ico, and an Xcode-ready iOS icon set from one image
- 100% offline processing — no uploads, no cloud
- Finder integration (Quick Actions and the Services menu)
- Drag & drop
- Watched folders / hot folder monitoring
- Clipboard image optimization
- Batch resize (6 modes: percentage, fixed width, fixed height, max dimensions, exact dimensions, max file size)
- Color space conversion (sRGB, Display P3, Adobe RGB, ProPhoto RGB, Rec. 709, Rec. 2020, Grayscale, CMYK)
- DPI metadata control (72–600 or custom)
- Video subtitle burn-in (SRT/VTT) and soft subtitle track preservation
- Video playback speed adjustment (0.25×–4×, pitch-preserved) and a resolution cap
- Video watermarks (PNG overlay, 9-anchor positioning, opacity, presets)
- Metadata control (strip all, keep essential, keep all, or Custom by category) plus your own copyright tags
- Shortcuts app integration, Raycast extension, and the command line tool (picmal-cli)
- Menu bar background operation
- Before/after preview slider
- Six quality presets plus custom
