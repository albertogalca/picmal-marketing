title: "Colour space conversion and FLAC to AAC fix"
pubDate: 2025-11-16
---

You can now convert images to a different colour space — sRGB, Display P3, Adobe RGB, ProPhoto RGB, Rec. 709 or Rec. 2020 — with proper ICC profile embedding. A new clickable icon in the Size column lets you view colour space info for converted images.

We also fixed FLAC to AAC audio conversion issues in optimised advanced mode.
