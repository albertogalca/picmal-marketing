title: "Batch image resize, media metadata and advanced mode"
pubDate: 2025-11-01
---

You can now batch resize images with five flexible modes: percentage, fixed dimensions, max dimensions, width-only or height-only.

Audio and video files now display metadata — duration, bitrate, codec, sample rate — and you get advanced mode with enhanced FFmpeg controls for both. PDF compression colours have been fixed for Keynote and PowerPoint presentations, and compressed files now automatically include a "\_compressed" suffix.

Format support has also been expanded to 31 audio and 25 video formats.
