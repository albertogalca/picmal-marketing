title: "Clipboard optimization, improved WebP to GIF, and DPI controls"
pubDate: 2026-02-18
---

Now with Picmal, animated WebP files convert to GIF with every frame preserved, and static WebP files convert smoothly without random errors. The whole process is more reliable, so you can trust the output whether you are working with simple images or full animations.

On top of that, Picmal now optimizes images the moment you copy them. As soon as something hits your clipboard, it gets compressed automatically and a small overlay appears so you can instantly grab the lighter version and keep working without breaking your flow.

And to give you even more control over your exports, you can now set the DPI metadata on output images. Choose from 72, 96, 144, 150, 300, 600, or enter your own custom value. This updates the resolution information for print or display workflows without changing the actual image dimensions.
