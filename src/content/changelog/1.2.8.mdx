title: "Conversion presets, metadata stripping and HEIC to WebP"
pubDate: 2025-11-30
---

This release brings audio and video conversion presets so you can save your favourite settings and reuse them with one click. HEIC to WebP conversion is now supported, and you can strip metadata from any file type — images, audio, and video — before exporting.

The output panel now shows which settings were used for each file, so you always know exactly how a conversion was produced. Under the hood, large parts of the codebase have been rewritten for better stability, and the Preferences menu has been reorganised for easier navigation.

We also fixed an issue where MKV to MP4 conversions with certain codecs produced unusable files.
