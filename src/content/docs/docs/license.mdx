## Activate your license

1. Open **Settings** → **License**
2. Enter your license key
3. Click **Activate**

Your license is tied to your Mac and stored securely in the keychain. It works offline after the initial activation.

## Deactivate

If you're moving to a new Mac, deactivate your license first:

1. Open **Settings** → **License**
2. Click **Deactivate**

This frees up the license so you can activate it on another machine.

You can also deactivate remotely through Lemon Squeezy:

1. Go to [app.lemonsqueezy.com/my-orders](https://app.lemonsqueezy.com/my-orders)
2. Select your order
3. Select the license key
4. Click **Deactivate** next to the activation

## Lost your license key?

If you can't find your license key or the original email:

1. Go to [app.lemonsqueezy.com/my-orders](https://app.lemonsqueezy.com/my-orders)
2. Enter the email address you used when purchasing
3. You'll receive an email with a link to view your orders and license keys
