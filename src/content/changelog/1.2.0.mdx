Picmal now handles audio and video files. Over 50 audio formats are supported — including MP3, WAV, AAC, FLAC, OGG, OPUS and many more — along with 24 video formats including MP4, MOV, AVI, MKV, WebM, FLV and WMV.

Drop your media files into Picmal just like you would with images and convert them in bulk.
