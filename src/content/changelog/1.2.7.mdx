title: "Folder support, Finder context menu and sharper PDFs"
pubDate: 2025-11-20
---

PDF results are now clearer and sharper. You can open files from the top menu using File > Open or with Command+O.

Picmal now understands folders — drop a folder and it will gather everything inside, including subfolders, ready to convert. On the Finder context menu you'll see new options to convert or compress files with Picmal directly from the right-click menu.
