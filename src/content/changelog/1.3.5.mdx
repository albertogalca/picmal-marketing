title: "Drop files into the menu bar"
pubDate: 2026-01-23

You can now drop files and folders directly into the menu tray area to start a conversion without opening the main window. This is the fastest way to convert a handful of files — just drag, drop, and done.

We also fixed an issue where dragging files onto the Picmal dock icon didn't work on some setups, plus a few small improvements under the hood.
