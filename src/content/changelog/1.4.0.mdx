Picmal now lets you compare original and output images side by side. Click the eye icon on any processed file to open an interactive slider that shows exactly what changed — perfect for dialing in your quality settings.

Video compression also got more powerful. The CRF range now spans 0–51 for finer quality control, and a new frame rate setting lets you reduce fps (e.g. 60→30) to shrink file sizes even further.
