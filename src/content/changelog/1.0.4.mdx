title: "Metadata removal, better window resizing and AVIF fix"
pubDate: 2025-07-16
---

You can now remove metadata from your images during conversion — EXIF data, GPS coordinates, ICC profiles, XMP and IPTC data. This is useful for protecting privacy before sharing photos online.

Window resizing has been improved with smoother animations and more responsive layout adjustments. We also fixed a critical issue where AVIF images could become corrupted during batch conversion.
