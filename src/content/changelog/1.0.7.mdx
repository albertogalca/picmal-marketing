Set your preferred output formats once in settings and Picmal will automatically apply them every time you drag and drop files. No more selecting the same format over and over.

![Default format conversion settings](/images/changelog/1.0.7/default_format.png)

Dark mode is here — switch to it in Settings > General for comfortable viewing on fully dark Mac setups. The settings menu has also been redesigned with a cleaner, more intuitive interface as a foundation for upcoming features.

![Dark mode interface](/images/changelog/1.0.7/dark_mode.png)

![Dark mode setting](/images/changelog/1.0.7/dark_mode_setting.png)
