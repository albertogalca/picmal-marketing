_This went out to the Picmal newsletter on May 31, 2026. [Sign up](#newsletter-form) to get the next one._

May was a quiet, heads-down month. I shipped nine small releases instead of one big one, mostly fixes and a few things people had written in asking for. Thank you to everyone who sent feedback this month, a lot of what's below started as an email from one of you.

Here are the bigger ones.

## Schedule a batch for later

There's a clock button next to Convert (and Compress) now. Tap it, pick a start time, and Picmal waits until then. I use it for big overnight jobs so I'm not sitting there listening to the fan. The bottom bar shows what's queued, you get a notification when it finishes, and a schedule survives quitting and reopening the app.

![Scheduling a batch to start later](/images/docs/schedule.png)

## Save your own presets

Set up the compression settings you like, hit "Save as preset", give it a name, and it shows up next to the built-in ones the next time you compress. It works for images, audio, and video. It's a small thing, but it saves rebuilding the same settings every session.

## See how much space you saved

When a batch finishes, the bottom bar and the notification show the total space you freed, with a small comparison for fun, like "Freed up 9.66 GB · about a 4K movie 🎬".

![Space freed shown in the bottom bar after a batch](/images/docs/space_saved.png)

## Set a priority on any file

Right-click a file and set it to High, Normal, or Low. High-priority files get processed first, even in the middle of a batch, so the one you actually need doesn't have to wait its turn.

![Right-click menu with High, Normal, and Low priority](/images/docs/priority_right_click.png)

## Outputs can sit in a subfolder next to each source

Turn it on in Settings → Output, type a name like "compressed", and every result is written to `<sourceFolder>/<name>/`. Files dropped from different folders each get their own subfolder, so your originals stay untouched. It works for images, audio, and video, in both Convert and Compress.

![Output subfolder setting in Settings → Output](/images/changelog/1.5.4/output_subfolder.png)

## Embed album art into your audio

If you keep a cover image next to your tracks (cover.jpg, folder.jpg, front.jpg), Picmal can find it and embed it automatically into MP3, M4A, ALAC, and FLAC. There's also an option to skip files that already have artwork.

![Cover art automatically embedded into audio files](/images/docs/cover_art.png)

## A few smaller things

- You can now convert to ALAC (Apple Lossless), pick an audio bit depth, and there's a new "CD quality" FLAC preset.
- Videos show an estimated compressed size before you run them.
- Pause now stops files mid-conversion, not just the ones still waiting.
- Watermarks can scale to each image's height, so one preset fits photos of any size.
- RAW photos keep their camera metadata when converting (make, model, lens, exposure, date, GPS).
- You can pick the DPI when turning a PDF into JPG or PNG.
- Plus fixes for wide-gamut color spaces, old FLV files, big-batch stalls, and a launch crash on some Intel Macs.
