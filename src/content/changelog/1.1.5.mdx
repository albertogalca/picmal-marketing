SVG compression is now available, and PDFs now convert every page — not just the first one. Performance has been improved across the board and a drag-and-drop crash has been fixed.

You can now choose from six compression presets — maximum quality, high quality, balanced, web optimised, social media, or maximum compression — or create your own custom settings.
